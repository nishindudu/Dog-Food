Columns

Long format: one row per project × score. A project nobody reviewed still appears once with empty score columns, so the gaps are visible instead of silently missing. Criterion columns are derived from the data, because the rubric is not fixed — with the shipped fixtures they are criterion_functionality, criterion_innovation and criterion_quality.

project_id, project_external_id, title, team, track, status, duplicate_of, submitted_at, criterion_<name>…, score_total, score_average, review_count, judge_id, judge_external_id, judge_email, judge_name, comment

score_total and score_average are the plain sum and mean of one judge's criteria. They are not normalized across judges and they are not weighted: that is T2 work and it is not claimed.

Getting it with curl

The checker attaches an organizer header and expects a 200 with a comma in the first line: 

curl -i \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <organizer token>" \
  http://localhost:8080/api/v1/export.csv

Tokens are printed when the portal boots and written into .dogfood.toml by python3 backend/seed.py --write-config.
